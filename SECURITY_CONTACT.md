# Private Security Disclosure Channel

For private follow-up on security vulnerabilities, please contact the maintainers directly via:

- **Email**: security@stellarlend.dev
- **Keybase**: [stellarlend-security](https://keybase.io/team/stellarlend-security)

Do not disclose vulnerabilities publicly until they have been addressed and approved for disclosure by the maintainers.

## Bug Bounty Payouts
If your report is approved for a bounty, you will be contacted privately to provide your USDC wallet address for payout. Ensure you use a secure and verified channel for this communication.

For urgent matters, include `[URGENT]` in the subject line of your email or Keybase message.
